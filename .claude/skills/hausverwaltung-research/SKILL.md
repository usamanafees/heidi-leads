# Researching a Hausverwaltung

## What matters for the lead score
1. **Units managed** (Wohneinheiten / Einheiten / verwaltete Wohnungen). The biggest driver of the score.
2. **Employees** (Mitarbeiter). Used to estimate size when units are not published.
3. **VDIV membership** (Verband der Immobilienverwalter Deutschland, incl. regional VDIV Berlin-Brandenburg).
4. **Decision-makers**: Geschäftsführer / Inhaber first, then heads of billing (Abrechnung, Betriebskosten, Umlagen), Prokurist, WEG lead.

## Where to look, in order
1. The company's own site: "Über uns", "Team", "Karriere", "Leistungen", Impressum.
2. Job ads (StepStone, Indeed, the company's careers page): they often state "wir verwalten rund 2.500 Einheiten" and team size.
3. Company registers and aggregators (Northdata, Creditreform entries): managing directors, Prokura.
4. Association member lists: VDIV Berlin-Brandenburg.
5. Press and local news.

## Reading German numbers
- "2.500" means 2500. "über 1.000" means 1000. "rund 3.000 Einheiten" means 3000.
- "WE" = Wohneinheiten (residential units).
- Count units, not buildings ("Objekte", "Liegenschaften" are buildings).

## Rules
- Only record what a page you actually opened or saw in search results says. Always pass that page's URL as `source_url` and a short verbatim quote.
- If sources disagree, prefer the company's own site, and mention the conflict in your final notes.
- Do not record an info@ address as a person's email.
- A LinkedIn profile only counts if name and employer both match.
